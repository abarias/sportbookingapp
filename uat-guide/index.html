User acceptance testing

Test the work, not the code.

UAT Home

Prove the booking experience works for the people who run it.

User acceptance testing is the business team’s final check that real tasks, permissions, prices, schedules, and handoffs behave as agreed. Follow the steps exactly, use synthetic UAT data, and record what actually happens.

0% executed

Before You Start

Prepare a clean, coordinated test run.

1. Agree the environment

Confirm the UAT URL, build/commit, database, storage buckets, email sender, cron schedule, payment mode, and test window with the environment owner.

2. Reserve synthetic identities

Assign one UAT-prefixed account per persona and two customer accounts for concurrency. Share passwords through an approved secret channel, never in this portal.

3. Divide the inventory

Allocate facilities, dates, and hours to testers. Record every handoff so a pricing, block, payment, or reschedule test does not invalidate another test.

4. Capture evidence

For failures, capture the full page, visible error, URL, time, browser, booking/reference ID, and console/network detail if your coordinator requests it.

Do not use real personal or payment data.Never upload a real receipt, enter a real transfer reference, or reuse a customer’s email/mobile number.

Issue Reporting Guide

Make every defect reproducible.

Critical

Security/data exposure, double booking, incorrect payment confirmation, destructive data loss, or complete service outage.

High

A major persona cannot finish a core booking, payment, facility, role, or rescheduling task.

Medium

A workaround exists, but validation, responsive behavior, or operational clarity is materially affected.

Low

Cosmetic wording/alignment issue with no business or accessibility impact.
